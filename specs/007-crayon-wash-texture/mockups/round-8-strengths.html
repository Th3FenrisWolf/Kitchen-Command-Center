Method

Make the ragu

Brown the beef and pork hard, in batches, so it fries rather than steams. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the fat, add the tomato paste and cook it until it darkens. Pour in the wine, scrape the pan, then the tomatoes and stock. Simmer it low, lid ajar, for two hours, stirring now and then, until it is thick enough to hold a line drawn through it with a spoon.

Below, the same wash capped at 1,000px, the largest the approved round showed. If the large outline reads wrong, the cap is the alternative; the phone card above shows the smallest wash.

Large, about 1,075px, no cap

Method

Braise the short ribs

Salt the ribs a day ahead. Sear them hard on every side, lift them out, then soften the shallots and garlic in the fat. Add the wine, reduce it by half, return the ribs with stock to come halfway up, cover and braise at 160 degrees for three hours, until the meat gives at the touch of a spoon. Skim, reduce the liquor to a gloss and spoon it over.
