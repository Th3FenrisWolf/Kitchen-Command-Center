Method

Make the ragu

Brown the beef and pork hard, in batches, so it fries rather than steams. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the fat, add the tomato paste and cook it until it darkens. Pour in the wine, scrape the pan, then the tomatoes and stock. Simmer it low, lid ajar, for two hours, stirring now and then, until it is thick enough to hold a line drawn through it with a spoon.
