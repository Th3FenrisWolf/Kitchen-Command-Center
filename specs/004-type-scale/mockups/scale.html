Brown Butter Gnocchi with Sage and Lemon

Nonna's Sunday version, halved.

Pillowy potato gnocchi in nutty brown butter, with crisp sage and a little lemon to lift it. The dough comes together in ten minutes.

Method

  1. 01

    Bake the potatoes whole until a knife slides through, then peel them while hot and pass them through a ricer.

  2. 02

    Work in the yolk and the flour with your fingertips until the dough just holds. Roll it into ropes and cut it into pillows.

  3. 03

    Brown the butter with the sage, then toss the boiled gnocchi through it and finish with lemon.
