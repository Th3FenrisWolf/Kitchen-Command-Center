## Control fills (approved 2026-11-18)

`control-fills.html`, with `control-fills-light.jpg` and `control-fills-dark.jpg`.

The round showed the proposed recipe page three times: the merged title and At a glance sheet with the notebook top,
Add Variant at its top-right, the Top variant card with the notebook top and a margin rule, and the variant tools
sheet. The three differed only in which controls on a sheet keep their paper fill.

**Approved: Nothing filled.** Every control on a sheet drops its paper fill: fields, ghost buttons, segmented
controls and badges. The rules and washes run through all of them. One condition from the owner: the text of every
button sits on a rule. In the round the filter pills' labels sit about 6px below the rule, so a rule crosses the
letters. The build must move each control's label onto a rule wherever the fill is gone (FR-020a).

**Not chosen:**

- Buttons keep paper: ghost buttons keep their fill. The owner chose one treatment for every control instead.
- Only fields clear: closest to today. The washes still stop at every pill and segment.

**Still open:** nothing. The notebook top, the merged sheet and the tools sheet drew no comment in this round, and the
build follows the round's shapes for them. The mockup's band, sized by hand in whole rules per sheet, is a sketch of
the look and not the build's method.
