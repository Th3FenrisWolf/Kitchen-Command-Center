Two steps

Cards, panels, forms and empty states turn 1.5° and grow by 1.5%, as in round 2. The two wide sheets, the pad's page and the hero, take one fixed smaller turn of 0.5° and grow by 0.5%. At that turn the pad's ends tip out about 5px, the same as a card's.

Wins on keeping what the owner picked: everything but the pad and the hero stays exactly at 1.5°. Costs: the visible edge still grows with width (a panel tips about 8px and a form about 7px, against a card's 5px), and the hero, at 3px, shows less than a card. Any new wide surface has to be marked wide by hand.

Card 392px · 1.5° · 1.015 · Panel and empty state 602px · 1.5° · 1.015 · Form 502px · 1.5° · 1.015

Pad page 1232px · 0.5° · 1.005 · Hero 702px · 0.5° · 1.005

Production: CSS only, with a custom property per surface. The pile sets the turn and scale by default, and a wide modifier on the pad's page and the hero sets the smaller pair. No container and no typed arithmetic are needed, so it works in every browser.

FR-003's cap moves from ±1° to ±1.5°.
