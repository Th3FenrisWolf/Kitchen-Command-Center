# Look rounds

Captures from `yarn perf:capture`, before at `97c51247` (the last commit before the first look-changing fix) and after
on this branch, compared with `yarn perf:diff`. The owner judges by eye; the diff share only points the eye.

## Round 1: every page type, desktop

Approved 2026-10-26: every page type looks the same on desktop, in both ramps, bare and bloomed, in Chromium and
WebKit. Chromium differs by 0% on every page. WebKit differs by up to 1.4% (home and sign-in), and by under 0.5% on
the static pages.

Phone: approved 2026-10-27 from the first phone set. That set repeated the top of each long page and left lower
cards blank in both refs; the owner judged what it showed as enough.

## Round 2: the washes after load, Chromium desktop

Approved 2026-10-27. Before the change, Chromium repainted every wash whose bloom had finished once hydration ran a
style recalc, so no page settled on its first paint (largest channel delta 86 to 133 of 255). A wash now rests at
its bloom's end and fills backwards only, so a finished bloom leaves no animation applied. Settled pages match the
first paint on 7 of 13 Chromium pairs; the rest differ by at most 30, in the header bar, and on search-1 in the
search panel. The first paint is unchanged on 11 pages and moves on search-1 (up to 57) and the 404 page (up to 95).

The header-bar residue was accepted as engine noise on 2026-10-27; the scripts-off pairs are drawn in SwiftShader.

## Bloom and tear round

Approved 2026-10-27, both options. The stepped bloom (45 held steps through `--wash-ease`) reads the same as the
old cubic-bezier curve at 90, 60 and 20 seconds. The halved-point tear (40 points a side on the standard sheets, 80
on the heroes) reads the same as the full tear on all eight presets at card and wide sizes, so it ships. The round
used hand-picked wash values and showed no tile tears; the tiles keep their 24 points.

## Round 3: baked washes against the live filter

Approved 2026-10-27. The baked washes (a grain tile and eight blob masks, generated by `yarn washes`, inks
pre-saturated per ramp) read the same as the live `#kcc-bloom` filter in both ramps and both engines. They give up the
grain's colour speckle, the displaced wobble along the ink edge and the free border-radius shapes.

## Round 4: wash strength

Chosen 2026-10-27 on real pages. Light: .42 of three (.55 now, .48, .42), because the washes read slightly too
strong. Dark: .18 of three (.16 now, .18 with soft ink at L .76, .20 with soft ink at L .78), because they read
slightly too faint. Dark washes sat at the AA floor for soft ink, and more saturation added no visibility, so the
stronger dark wash lifts the soft ink to keep 4.5:1.

## Round 5: texture files against inline textures

Approved 2026-10-27. The baked textures moved out of the stylesheet into nine cached, preloaded PNG files, which takes
the CSS gzip back under its baseline. Settled captures of every page in both ramps and both engines are identical to
the inline textures. The move exposed a focus-ring fade that ringed every control on the scripts-off first paint of
sign-in; the fade now runs only once the page has focus.
