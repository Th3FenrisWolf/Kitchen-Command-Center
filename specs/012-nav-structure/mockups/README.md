# 012 Navigation Structure: Look Rounds

## Desktop header: Menus over meal tabs (approved)

`nav.html`, `nav-light.jpg`, `nav-dark.jpg`.

Browse, Cook and Plan are menu buttons in the bar, where Recipes is today. The meal categories hang below the pad's
right edge as divider tabs with counts, ending in All, one click each to a filtered list. Guests get Sign in as a
ghost button and Join in marker green beside it. The light/dark toggle is an icon button between search and the
account buttons. Plan (meal plan, shopping list, household) is shown to members only, dashed and marked soon.

It won because it keeps the meals one click away, as the divider tabs of the first sketch did, while Browse, Cook
and Plan give the nav a structure that later features (Favorites, Households) can join.

The other options in round 1 (`.mockups/012-nav-structure/round-1-browse-cook-plan.html`, local only):

- **Task tabs**: Browse, Cook and Plan hung below the pad as divider tabs, and the bar kept only logo, search, the
  toggle and the account buttons. The owner picked Menus over meal tabs instead.
- **Dividers with sub-tabs**: section tabs along the pad with a second row of sub-tabs for the current section. The
  owner picked Menus over meal tabs instead.

The round-1 mockup draws the tabs with plain shadows, omits washes, and shows the open card below the tab row
rather than tucked behind the pad.

## Phone menu: Divider-tabbed menu (approved)

`phone.html`, `phone-light.jpg`, `phone-dark.jpg`.

The phone bar keeps the logo, the search button and Menu. The open Menu card carries Browse, Cook and Plan divider
tabs along its top edge, with the chosen tab pulled forward, and shows only that section above the account foot.
Browse holds Meals and Diets as two-column icon lists with counts, Quick picks and All recipes. Cook holds Recently
viewed, Last cooked and Surprise me. Plan is members only, dashed and marked soon. The Dark/Light switch sits in the
card's account foot. Guests get Sign in and Join in the foot.

It won because it keeps the phone menu short and mirrors the desktop's Browse, Cook and Plan.

The other options in round 2 (`.mockups/012-nav-structure/round-2-phone.html`, local only):

- **Stacked sections**: every section in one long scroll. The owner picked the tabbed menu instead.
- **Meal tabs under the bar**: a sideways-scrolling meal tab strip under the phone pad, like desktop. The owner
  picked the tabbed menu instead, so the phone has no meal tab strip.

The round-2 mockup draws the divider tabs inside the card's top edge, because the card's top is tucked under the pad.
